# Aificient Studio — User Guide

Welcome to **[Aificient Studio](https://aificient.io/)**. This guide explains how to
turn a single line of text into a finished, faceless AI video and publish it to
TikTok, Reels & Shorts. It also grounds the in-app support assistant, so keeping it
accurate helps every user.

## 1. Install and sign in

1. [Download Aificient Studio](https://aificient.io/download) for macOS or Windows.
2. Launch the app and sign in with Google or with an email magic code.
3. On first run, choose where videos render: your **local GPU**, or a **rented GPU**
   in the cloud. See the [local AI video generator](https://aificient.io/local-ai-video-generator)
   page for hardware notes.

## 2. Create your first video

1. **Write the idea.** Type a single line describing the video you want.
2. **Concept or Brainstorm.** Pick *Concept* to go straight to a finished plan, or
   *Brainstorm* to explore ideas first.
3. **Generate the scene.** Aificient writes the script, generates the visuals, adds a
   voice, and lays in sound effects.
4. **Review and export.** Tweak any scene, then export the finished video.

Aificient Studio is a [faceless video generator](https://aificient.io/faceless-video-generator):
no camera, no face, no editing timeline required.

## 3. Characters and voices

- **Recurring characters.** Turn a real photo into a reusable character that appears
  across scenes.
- **Voices.** Choose male or female real-sounding voices, in English or Spanish.

## 4. Publish to TikTok, Reels & Shorts

Aificient is built as an [AI TikTok video generator](https://aificient.io/ai-tiktok-video-generator).
Export your video and post it, or use the [TikTok scheduler](https://aificient.io/tiktok-scheduler)
to publish on a schedule.

## 5. Captions and hashtags

Generate platform-specific captions and hashtags for Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube
directly from the project, in English or Spanish.

## 6. Cost and rendering

Run on your own GPU for the lowest cost, or rent one when you need more power — a wall
of posts for cents. Compare Aificient with other tools:
[AutoShorts alternative](https://aificient.io/autoshorts-alternative) ·
[SendShort alternative](https://aificient.io/sendshort-alternative).

## 7. Troubleshooting & support

- Check that your GPU drivers are up to date for local rendering.
- If a generation fails, retry; transient provider errors are common.
- Still stuck? [Contact the Aificient team](https://aificient.io/contact).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my face required?** No — Aificient Studio makes faceless videos.

**Where do my videos render?** On your local GPU or a rented cloud GPU, your choice.

**What platforms are supported?** TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

**Where can I learn more?** Visit [aificient.io](https://aificient.io/) or read the
[about page](https://aificient.io/about).
